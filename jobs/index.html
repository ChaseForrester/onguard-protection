Tier 1 — what we build first

  • Dedicated jobs page for available security positions.
  • Firebase Authentication: Google, email, and phone.
  • Guard-only gate. Anyone else is logged out.

Tier 2 — after 10 verified guards

  • Public roster with a short self-written bio.
  • Homepage search: location, time frame, experience, other filters.
